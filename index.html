What is the EF scale?

Nobody can easily measure the wind inside a tornado. So after a storm, experts study the damage and work backward to estimate how fast the wind was. That estimate is the Enhanced Fujita scale, used in the United States since 2007.

The speeds are 3‑second gusts. EF0 is the weakest. EF5 is the strongest, and it is rare.

    If a tornado warning is issued

    • Go to the lowest floor of a sturdy building.
    • Pick a small inside room with no windows, like a closet or bathroom.
    • Cover your head and neck.
    • Cars and mobile homes are not safe places to shelter.

    A simplified model for learning. Real damage depends on how things are built, how long the wind lasts, and what debris is flying. Descriptions are based on the Enhanced Fujita scale damage indicators used by the U.S. National Weather Service.
